Medical disclaimer

Effective 3 October 2026

Peptio is a personal logging and reminder tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

  • Peptio never suggests, calculates or recommends doses. Amounts in the app are the ones you enter.
  • Charts, patterns and observations describe what you recorded. They do not show cause and effect and are not a medical assessment.
  • Reminders can fail — for example if notifications are turned off or the device is off. Don't rely on Peptio as your only reminder for anything critical.
  • Always follow the instructions of your doctor, pharmacist or other qualified healthcare professional, and talk to them before starting, stopping or changing any medication or compound.
  • If you think you may have a medical emergency, contact your local emergency services immediately.
